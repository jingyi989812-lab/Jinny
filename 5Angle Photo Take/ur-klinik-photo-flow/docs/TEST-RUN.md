# Test run checklist

Prerequisite: Eric has finished steps 1–9 of `FOR-ERIC.md`.

**Use a volunteer who agrees to be photographed, e.g. a staff member with a customer record.**
Every photo goes into that person's real folder.

## At the outlet, on the iPhone 6

| # | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Photo Flow from the Home Screen | Opens in a few seconds; green "Connected — photos save to the NAS" |
| 2 | Today's Customers → tap your outlet | Only your outlet's bookings, with times. "arrived" beside checked-in customers. |
| 3 | Tap the volunteer | Confirm screen shows their name, ID, and "Photo folder: BM …" — **check it is the right folder** |
| 4 | Yes → Take Photo → take FRONT in the camera → Use Photo | Photo check passes, moves to L 45 |
| 5 | Take the other 4 angles (or take all 5 in the Camera app, then **Import from Photos**) | "BEFORE PHOTOS COMPLETE", 5 thumbnails the right way up, left/right not swapped |
| 6 | Save Before Photos | "SAVED", destination shows the volunteer's folder |
| 7 | Start After Photos → 5 angles → Save | "ALL PHOTOS SAVED — 10 photos" |
| 8 | On a PC, open File Station | `New 5 Angle Photo\<their folder>\<today>\BEFORE` and `AFTER`, 5 photos each, full size (about 2–3 MB, 3264×2448), plus `_reference.jpg` |
| 9 | Back in the app: Today's Customers | Volunteer shows "Complete"; their reference photo appears |
| 10 | Search → type part of the volunteer's name, then their number | Found both ways |
| 11 | New Walk-in → type an ID that **already exists** | "Membership ID in use" with that folder and photo — then go back |
| 11 | Tap the volunteer again → **+ ADD OTHERS (HAND, LEG, BACK…)** → type `hand` → 2 photos → Done → Save | In File Station: `6-HAND-01_…` beside the five angles |
| 12 | Start their AFTER session | It reminds you: "HAND — 2 before, not taken yet" |
| 13 | Another volunteer → **OTHERS — HAND, LEG…** → label `left leg` → 2 photos → Save | Saved as `1-LEFT-LEG-01_…`, no face angles |
| 14 | A volunteer with no photos today → **AFTER ›** (bottom right) → 5-Angle Photo → Save | Only an `AFTER` folder is made — the doctor's "after only" case |
| 15 | Sign out, sign in as a therapist from another outlet | Only that outlet's customers are listed |
| 16 | Turn the wifi **off** → take 5 angles → Save | "Couldn't reach the NAS", and "5 photos are saved on this phone" |
| 17 | Close the app completely, turn the wifi back **on**, open the app | The home screen shows them waiting, then they upload by themselves — check File Station |
| 18 | Today's Customers → type part of a name in **Find in today's list** | Only that customer shows |
| 12 | Turn wifi off → open Today's Customers | "No connection to the server" — no fake customers, nothing pretends to save |
| 13 | Wifi on, Home → tap the orange status | Back to "Connected" |
