# Mystery Shopper, September 2026: Presenter Script

About 4 to 5 minutes in total. The same text is in the deck's speaker notes (PowerPoint: Slide Show > Presenter View).

**1. Cover**
Good morning everyone. Today I'll share our Mystery Shopper results for September 2026. One shopper visited each of our 3 outlets. Puchong got our top score at 93.25, Greenlane got 77.5, and Cheras Leisure Mall got 66.75.

**2. At a glance**
Here's a quick look at all three. On top is what each shopper loved, and at the bottom is where we can grow. The good news: every shopper found something they liked. One thing to note: price came up in all three visits.

**3. Greenlane (77.5)**
Greenlane scored 77.5. The shopper was impressed by our equipment and loved the small details: the travel set gift, the warm red date tea, and a clean, tidy clinic. Sylvin was praised for being very friendly. Well done, Sylvin! To grow: the shopper wanted the AI analysis to feel more interactive, with more of a "wow". They also hoped the visit could be shorter, within 2 hours, and felt the price could be more accessible.

**4. Puchong (93.25)**
Puchong is our top score at 93.25. Congratulations, team! The shopper said the overall experience was very good. The doctor explained everything clearly, and the therapist was friendly and caring. Just a few small touches: during treatment, remind customers of simple actions like turning the head. After the bright light, help their eyes adjust before they look in the mirror. And the price may feel high for some customers.

**5. Cheras Leisure Mall (66.75)**
Cheras Leisure Mall scored 66.75. The good part: the customer was happy with the result, and her skin looked brighter. Where we can improve: she felt pushed to buy products or packages, and the price felt too high. She also hoped for less waiting and a quieter corridor. And she prefers WhatsApp messages instead of phone calls.

**6. What the shoppers experienced**
In one line each: Greenlane, impressive technology and thoughtful details. Puchong, clear explanation and caring treatment. Cheras, a good visible result. Please take the improvement points back to your team.

**7. In the customers' own words**
Here are some of the shoppers' own words, with English translations. *(Read one or two out loud.)*

**8. Thank you**
Thank you, team. Our shoppers remembered the warm welcome, the clear explanations and the caring hands. Let's keep that going, one visit at a time. Any questions?
