# Mystery Shopper Improvement Tracker: Meeting Script

About 2 minutes. Read while showing the tracker on screen.

## English

**1. Why we have this**
From our internal audit, one improvement point was that we need to track Mystery Shopper feedback properly, from the feedback all the way until the problem is fixed. So QA has set up this tracker.

**2. How it works**
One card is one improvement point. Each card follows the same steps: feedback, improvement point, action plan, PIC, target date, follow-up, verify, and close.

**3. The numbers at the top**
These show at a glance how many points are Open, in progress, being monitored, overdue, verified and closed.

**4. What we have now**
We have 7 points so far: 1 from Greenlane and 6 from Cheras Leisure Mall. Two of them are linked under the same theme, Consultant Sales Approach. At Greenlane, the customer felt the attitude changed because of her budget. At Cheras, the customer felt the package talk was too commercial. They are different cases, but the same area to improve.

**5. What we need today**
For each point, please help us agree on three things: the action plan, the PIC, and the target date.

**6. After today**
The PIC carries out the action. At every meeting, we record what was done, when, and the evidence. QA then checks the next Mystery Shopper report. If the problem doesn't come back, we verify and close it. If it comes back, we reopen it.

**7. Closing**
The Summary tab shows what we found, what we did, and whether it has improved, so we can answer any audit question quickly. Any questions?

## 中文

**1. 为什么要做这个**
在我们的内部稽核里，有一个改进点是：神秘顾客的反馈需要好好跟进，从收到反馈一直到问题真正改善。所以 QA 做了这个追踪系统。

**2. 怎么运作**
一张卡就是一个改进点。每张卡都按照同样的步骤：反馈、改进点、行动计划、负责人、目标日期、跟进、验证、结案。

**3. 上面的数字**
一眼就能看到有多少个是 Open、进行中、观察中、逾期、已验证和已结案。

**4. 目前的内容**
目前一共有 7 个改进点：Greenlane 1 个，Cheras Leisure Mall 6 个。其中两个归在同一个主题“顾问销售方式”下面。Greenlane 的顾客觉得知道她预算有限后，服务态度变了；Cheras 的顾客觉得介绍配套太商业。虽然是不同的个案，但都是同一个需要改进的方向。

**5. 今天需要大家帮忙的**
每个改进点，请大家一起决定三件事：行动计划、负责人（PIC）、目标日期。

**6. 之后怎么做**
负责人去执行行动。每次开会，我们记录做了什么、什么时候做的、有什么证据。QA 会看下一次的神秘顾客报告：如果问题没有再出现，就验证并结案；如果又出现，就重新打开。

**7. 结尾**
“Summary”页面可以直接看到我们发现了什么、做了什么、有没有改善，稽核时可以很快回答。大家有什么问题吗？
